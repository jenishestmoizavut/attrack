Attendance Tracker for College Students

Attrack is a free attendance tracker designed for college students. It helps track daily lectures, calculate attendance percentage, and shows how many classes you can miss while staying above the target attendance. The app works offline and does not require login.

Check your subject-wise buffer

Your college tracks attendance per subject, not just overall — so Attrack now does too. Head to Subject overview and tap any subject to see its own attendance % and exactly how many lectures you can still skip in it.

New: subject-wise targets 🎯

Your college probably tracks attendance per subject, not overall — so now Attrack does too:

  • Give any subject its own attendance target (in Setup, or when adding a subject) — handy for labs vs. lectures.
  • Tap a subject in "Subject overview" to see exactly how many of that subject's lectures you can still skip.
